Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how the app Barely Payday (Italian title: Arriva a fine mese, "the App") handles user data. The App is published by Emanuele Tani Borsari ("the Developer"), who can be reached at e.tani@hotmail.it.

1. Data the App stores on your device

Game progress (current game, records, medals, daily challenge, chosen language) is stored only on your device. The Developer does not receive it or keep it on any server. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this data is deleted.

2. Data collected by the Developer

The Developer does not collect personal data: the App requires no sign-up, account, location, contacts, photos or microphone.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos after a mini-game and a video at the end of a year. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process, as independent controllers, data such as the device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and App usage information. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the App shows a consent form (Google UMP) before personalized ads: you can accept, decline or change your choice at any time from the App or your device settings (Google → Ads), where you can also reset or disable your advertising ID.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

5. Your rights

Because the Developer does not collect personal data, there is none to correct or delete. For data processed by Google you can exercise your rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection, portability; complaint to your data protection authority) under Google's policies. For any question write to e.tani@hotmail.it.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated, for example if new features are added. The date above shows the latest version.
